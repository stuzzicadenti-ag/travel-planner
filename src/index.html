👤

Plan me 3 days in Rome, focusing on history and food.

🤖

Here's your personalized Rome itinerary:

☀ Day 1 — Ancient Rome

09:00
Colosseum & Roman Forum

Start early to beat the crowds. Book skip-the-line tickets in advance. Allow 2-3 hours to explore the amphitheater and the sprawling ruins of the Forum.

13:00
Lunch at Rione Monti

Try cacio e pepe at a local trattoria in Rome's oldest neighborhood.

15:30
Trevi Fountain & Spanish Steps

Toss a coin, make a wish. Best visited in late afternoon when the light is golden. Walk up to the Steps for a panoramic view.

🌈 Day 2 — Vatican City

08:00
Vatican Museums & Sistine Chapel

Arrive at opening. The Sistine Chapel ceiling by Michelangelo is breathtaking. Don't rush—spend at least 3 hours here.

12:00
St. Peter's Basilica

Free entry. Climb the dome for the best view of Rome. Tip: the last 320 steps are narrow—worth every one.

15:00
Castel Sant'Angelo & gelato

Walk along the Tiber to this ancient fortress. Stop for artisan gelato at Fatamorgana nearby.

🌝 Day 3 — Local Life

10:00
Trastevere Morning Walk

Wander cobblestone streets, discover hidden piazzas, and soak in Rome's most charming neighborhood.

12:30
Mercato di Testaccio

Rome's best food market. Try supplì (fried rice balls) and fresh pasta. A local foodie paradise.

15:00
Pantheon & Piazza Navona

The Pantheon's 2,000-year-old dome is an engineering marvel. End the trip at Piazza Navona with an espresso.

💡 Tip: Get a Roma Pass for free public transport and discounted museum entries. Best time to visit: April-May or September-October.
